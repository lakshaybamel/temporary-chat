Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Anonymous Chat

No accounts, no profiles. Every guest gets a random identity for the life of the room.

Real-time Messaging

Messages land instantly over WebSockets, so conversations feel like being in the same room.

File Sharing

Drop in a file up to 10 MB and share it as a clean, downloadable card in the chat.

QR / Link Sharing

Share a room instantly with a scannable QR code or a single tappable link.

Temporary Rooms

Every room runs on a countdown. When time's up, the room and everything in it disappears.

Message History

Catch up instantly — anyone who joins mid-conversation sees the full history so far.

About

Not every conversation needs to last forever

Fleeting exists for the chats and files that don't need a permanent home — a quick coordination with a stranger, a one-off file handoff, a conversation you'd rather not leave sitting in an inbox. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing left behind: once a room expires, its messages and files are gone for good.
